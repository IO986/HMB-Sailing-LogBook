=== English (en-US) ===
• 3 new languages: Croatian, Slovenian, Italian (COLREG in English)
• Marine instruments: a note that the connection needs the boat's WiFi with an NMEA gateway
• The prompt to connect instruments now appears once, not on every launch
• Added translations: map (new waypoint), MOB, Mayday card, Morse, log interval, About
• Fixed the name of an unnamed waypoint ("Waypoint 12:5")
