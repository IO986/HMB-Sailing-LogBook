HMB Sailing Log — build 75 (1.37.0+75)

Play Console limit: 500 characters per language.

=== English (en-US) ===
• New: anchor and MOB on the map. Tap the anchor to start the watch at your position, hold it to guard a drawn area. Hold the red button for man overboard.
• New: both work without tracking, and the entry lands under the right date.
• New: anchor alarm takes any sound from your phone, at a volume that no longer depends on the phone's own setting.
• New: the swing on the chain is drawn on the map and summed up in the day.
• New: mile certificates are signed before export, with a checksum and QR.
