=== English (en-US) ===
• Fixed: on first launch the Continue button on the language screen could not be tapped — it looked disabled, and in landscape the list would not scroll either
• The anchor alarm radius now goes up to 70 m (was 30 m), enough for a longer scope in deeper water

• Polmer alarma sidra zdaj sega do 70 m (prej 30 m), dovolj za daljšo verigo v globlji vodi

=== Italiano (it-IT) ===
